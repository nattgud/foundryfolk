Återanvändbar Prompt för Stilbeskrivning
Kärnbeskrivning (Börja alltid med denna)
An illustrated, detailed isometric logo or game asset, presented in a "2D/3D hybrid" fantasy-steampunk art style. The asset must have bold, clean, heavy black outlines, making it look like a high-quality hand-drawn piece with tangible, sculpted volume.

Tematisk och Materiell Estetik
The object must be composed of weathered, hand-chiseled stone masonry and textured, cast-metal gears and components (iron, bronze, copper). The overall theme is a blend of ancient Dwarven stonework, magical power, and primitive industrial engineering. All surfaces must show tactile, rustic wear, but remain clean and defined.

Färgpalett och Textur
Base Colors: Deep charcoal gray (#2D2D2D), dark chiseled stone gray, rustic cast iron, and aged bronze.
Accent Colors: Vibrant, glowing magical cyan and teal for particle effects and internal light sources.
Primary Colors (for focal points): Rich crimson, gold/ochre, and copper.
Internal Illumination: The core of the object must emit a warm, powerful, pulsing orange-gold glow, contrasting sharply with cool magical light sources. Surfaces must feature intricate, glowing etched runes.

Specifik Detaljnivå (Från logotypen)
The design must incorporate several specific elements that define the theme: intricate mechanical gears of various sizes; stylized runic filigree etched into stone surfaces; powerful tools like hammers and anvils; small clusters of glowing magical crystals or cyan particle trails; and robust, chiseled stone block text.

Komposition och Ljussättning
Composition: Symmetrical and centered, with a strong, solid base. Elements must be layered, not flat, to give a sense of deep construction.
Lighting: Dramatic and focused. Internal light from a forge or crystal core is the primary source, casting defined, high-contrast light and shadow on the metal and stone surfaces.
Edges: All outlines must be razor-sharp and black, defining every block, gear, and text character without any blur or atmospheric glow.
Background: A smooth, uniform background with high contrast (e.g., solid gray or a greenscreen-ready color like #00FF00) to emphasize the hard edges.

Hur du återanvänder prompten för nya motiv
När du vill skapa en ny bild i samma stil, använder du styckena ovan och lägger bara till ditt konkreta motiv och specifika färgförändringar i början.

Exempel 1: En karaktär i stilen

"An illustrated, detailed isometric [character design: a Dwarven master blacksmith wearing heavy, ornate gear-punk goggles and leather apron], presented in a "2D/3D hybrid" fantasy-steampunk art style... [Klipp in resten av kärnbeskrivningen och Paletten, och lägg till 'The blacksmith should hold a hammer that glows with internal cyan magical energy']"

Exempel 2: Ett föremål i stilen

"An illustrated, detailed isometric [object: a powerful magical power coil with an ancient Dwarven runic base], presented in a "2D/3D hybrid" fantasy-steampunk art style... [Klipp in kärnbeskrivningen och byt Palettens crimson/gold till 'with dark copper coils and brilliant magenta electrical pulses', och lägg till 'The runic base should emit a powerful, cool blue internal illumination']"
